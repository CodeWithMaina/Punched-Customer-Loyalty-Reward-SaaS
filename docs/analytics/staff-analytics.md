# Staff Analytics Design

Audience: **Staff** (Role = Staff). Scope is strictly the staff's linked business
(`User.StaffBusinessId`). Staff see ONLY their own performance data. Business owners
see staff data via the business endpoints.

## Staff self-view dashboard (P0)

```
GET /v1/businesses/staff/analytics   → StaffAnalyticsResponse (already wired)
```

### Owner view of a single staff member (P0, already implemented)

```
GET /v1/businesses/me/staff/{staffId}/analytics?period=all
```
Returns `StaffMemberAnalyticsResponse`: stamps issued, customers served (period + all-time), recent activity.

**Extend with** (P1):

## Normalization & fairness

Never rank staff by raw stamp count alone — it is misleading when working schedules differ.
Use normalized metrics:

| Normalized metric | Formula | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Stamps per active day | `stamps / days with ≥1 stamp` | volume per working day |
| Customers served per stamp | `distinct customers / stamps` | efficiency of new-customer acquisition |
| Repeat-customer rate | `customers served 2+× / total customers served` | retention driven |
| Stamps per customer-session | aggregate | engagement depth |

> ⚠️ True "utilization %" (stamps / working-hours) is **NOT AVAILABLE** — there is no
> staff-shift/schedule table. This is a documented gap.

## Staff identification & attribution

- Staff identity = `User.Id` carried in the `userId` claim.
- Attribution on every stamp = `Stamp.AwardedByUserId` (set by `StampService.AwardStampAsync`
  using the authenticated caller's `userId`).
- **Gap:** `AwardedByUserId` was back-filled as nullable (migration
  `20260422_AddStampAttribution`). Stamps created before that migration have
  `AwardedByUserId = NULL`, so historical staff stats are understated. New flows are
  always attributed.

## Security

- Staff self-view scoped to `User.StaffBusinessId`.
- Staff may NOT see other staff's analytics (owner-only).
- Staff may NOT see business totals (only own activity).
- SSE subscription (`/v1/sse/cards/{cardId}`) is guarded: card must belong to the customer.
